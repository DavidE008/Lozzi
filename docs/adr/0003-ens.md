# ADR 0003: ENS subnames use an Ethereum Sepolia adapter

Status: Accepted

Hackathon ENS subnames are managed under an institution-controlled Ethereum
Sepolia parent through a provider adapter. Records contain routing identifiers
only—never names, emails, grades, student numbers, or other sensitive text.
ENS failure is non-blocking and reported through capability state.

For real activation, the parent and issued wrapped subnames are controlled by a
2-of-3 institutional Safe. A non-upgradeable, parent-bound adapter receives only
issuance authority, and its automated issuer uses a managed signer rather than a
raw production private key. Students receive a revocable forward-resolution
alias after wallet proof and explicit public-link consent; issuance does not set
a primary/reverse name or convey academic authority.

The implementation, rollout, recovery, and evidence gates are defined in the
[ENS real-integration plan](../integrations/ens-real-integration-plan.md). No
registration, deployment, approval, funding, or live write is authorized by
this ADR.
